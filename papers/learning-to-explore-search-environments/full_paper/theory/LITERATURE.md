# Novelty boundaries for the next experiment

**Verified 10 September 2026.** This is a targeted audit, not an exhaustive novelty search. All titles and stated abstract claims below were read from the linked primary abstract pages. GoBOED's full HTML introduction, method setup and Appendix H were also inspected. Blackwell's citation was verified through Crossref; the publisher blocked access, so the Gaussian comparison in THEORY.md is proved directly rather than attributed to an unread passage. No claim about priority is stronger than the recorded inspection supports.

| Source | Verified scope | Consequence for this project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Blackwell, *Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments*, Annals of Mathematical Statistics 24(2), 265–272, 1953](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032) ([verified metadata](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1214%2Faoms%2F1177729032)) | Author, title, venue and date verified through Crossref. Experiment comparison by decision performance is classical. | Do not call Blackwell ordering, Gaussian garbling, or utility-specific experiment comparison new. Our binary Gaussian proof is elementary. |
| [Javdani, Chen, Karbasi, Krause, Bagnell and Srinivasa, *Near Optimal Bayesian Active Learning for Decision Making*, AISTATS 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.5886) | Overlapping decision regions; necessary and sufficient consistency conditions; Hyperedge Cutting with competitiveness to optimal policies; robotics and comparison learning. | The proposed “decision partitions,” “conflict hypergraphs,” and active determination of a useful action are directly preceded. This is a required conceptual comparator. |
| [Degenne and Koolen, *Pure Exploration with Multiple Correct Answers*, 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.03475) | Game-based sample-complexity lower bound and an asymptotically matching extension of Track-and-Stop to multiple-answer problems. | Multiple correct routers and game-based identification complexity are existing topics. Do not introduce a minimax identification game as if none exists. Abstract verified; no theorem-by-theorem reduction claimed. |
| [Liu, Raghunathan, Liang and Finn, *Decoupling Exploration and Exploitation for Meta-Reinforcement Learning without Sacrifices*, ICML 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02790) | DREAM separates task-relevant exploitation and exploration objectives, explicitly addressing a chicken-and-egg problem and irrelevant exploration. | The original selector's zero rewards connect to an already recognized coupling problem. Search's source-label/target-document contract must carry the empirical distinction. |
| [Jin, Krishnamurthy, Simchowitz and Yu, *Reward-Free Exploration for Reinforcement Learning*, 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.02794) | Exploration without a specified reward, followed by near-optimal policies for many reward functions; efficient algorithm and near-matching sample-complexity lower bound. | Acquiring information before knowing the later task is not a new premise. Our fixed-search-service, synthetic-observation setting has a different utility-transfer problem, but needs an explicit comparison. |
| [Bickford Smith, Kirsch, Farquhar, Gal, Foster and Rainforth, *Prediction-Oriented Bayesian Active Learning*, AISTATS 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08151) | EPIG uses information about predictions, and explicitly criticizes parameter-information acquisition for lacking an input distribution. | “Weight exploration by the future query distribution” alone is not new. Compare prediction/decision entropy with utility loss, including uncertainty in that distribution. |
| [Go and Isaac, *Robust Expected Information Gain for Optimal Bayesian Experimental Design Using Ambiguity Sets*, UAI 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.09914) | REIG minimizes an affine EIG relaxation over KL ambiguity sets around a prior; computational stabilization and numerical tests. | Robustifying acquisition over a distribution set is established. Distinguish workload uncertainty from uncertainty in world priors and from sampling error. |
| [Go, Qian and Yoon, *Goal-driven Bayesian Optimal Experimental Design for Robust Decision-Making Under Model Uncertainty*, May 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26093) ([full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26093v1)) | GoBOED combines an amortized variational posterior with a differentiable constrained convex decision layer. Theorem 1/Appendix H establish null-subspace cancellation under task-relevant linear-subspace and pathwise differentiability assumptions. The implemented design is single-step, nonadaptive. | “Learn a decision-focused experiment model,” “ignore nuisance dimensions,” and “differentiate robust downstream decision loss” are already concrete competing methods. We cannot present these as fresh generic principles. This source must enter the next paper's related work. |
| [Grünwald and Halpern, *When Ignorance is Bliss*, UAI 2004](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0510080) | Discusses ignoring information with sets of probability measures, dilation, and some small-sample Bayesian prediction failures. | The max/expectation-order counterexample is a contract warning in an established area, not a new paradox of information. |

The closest new-paper claim worth testing is narrower: **a source-trained model predicts which automatically checkable search probes preserve useful routing choices across specified future workload shifts, and its uncertainty/capacity diagnostics forecast when adaptation will fail.** Earning it requires new held-out-family evidence; renaming standard VOI, a robust linear program, or DRD does not earn it.

Priority remains unresolved for the complete restricted combination: pre-query document probes; frozen search service; numeric persistent state; future context-shift robustness; a source-transport error model; complete deployment costs. This audit establishes that its individual ingredients are crowded, not that the combination is unique. A thorough next pass should read the closest methods in full and compare their formal information contracts and experimental capabilities.

The search also surfaced newer robust-design and reward-agnostic-exploration papers, but their full methods were not inspected, so they are follow-up leads rather than a basis for priority claims: [*Maximin Robust Bayesian Experimental Design*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14094), [*Robust Experimental Design via Generalised Bayesian Inference*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07671), and [*Improved Bounds for Reward-Agnostic and Reward-Free Exploration*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16363). Their titles/abstract snippets were observed on arXiv's search pages.
