What moved, and why

Title. "A Unifying Theory of LLM-Guided Artifact Search" → "Learning Beyond Gradients: Resources, Certificates, and Limits of LLM-Guided Artifact Search." Both reviewers held that "unifying theory" promised a theorem requiring the unified object that v1 did not deliver. v2 delivers one such theorem (below), but we also retreated from the contested word: the new title describes what the paper proves — per-resource laws (resources), soundness and certified-improvement guarantees (certificates), and Goodhart impossibility (limits).

The composed theorem (R1's Q2, R2's R9). New §6, Theorem 6.1 (certified improvement at the compound rate): under Assumption 3.1 (boost γ), a σ-subgaussian evaluator, the Prop. 5.2 gate at confidence δ/B, and a (Δ,ε)-separated landscape, the gated loop simultaneously makes no false accept and certifies an ε-good artifact within ⌈2 ln(1/δ)/(γq₀(Gε))⌉ decisions — total evaluator cost O((σ²/Δ²)·ln(B/δ)ln(1/δ)/(γq₀(Gε))). The false-reject slowdown R1 asked about appears explicitly as the (1−δ/B) factor. Full proof in Appendix E, following R2's sketch with the budget proviso made precise.

Operationalizing γ (R1's W2/Q1, R2's R10). Two additions. Proposition 3.5 proves the plug-in estimator γ̂ = q̂τ/q̂₀ is within relative error η of γ with probability 1−δ once K ≥ (9/2q²minη²)ln(4/δ) verified proposals per condition — Assumption 3.1 is now falsifiable at a known sample cost. Appendix I pre-registers the concrete experiment (120 buggy programs, K=70 patches per condition from one frozen open model, cold vs. stack-trace prompts, Wilson intervals, the falsifying outcome stated). We are explicit in §11 that the measurement has not yet been run; the protocol, its cost (~1 GPU-day), and its scaled-up version on web-agent harness search are specified to the level of prompts.

Honesty about sources (W8, Q6). Per the instruction that some cited works may not strictly support the claims: the load-bearing empirical anchors for §5 are now archival — Henderson et al. (AAAI 2018) on few-seed fragility, Dodge et al. (EMNLP 2019) on expected-max inflation (literally Thm 5.1(i) in deployed form), Agarwal et al. (NeurIPS 2021) on interval-based aggregation (Cor. 5.3 in benchmark form). The autoresearch 53%/11% episode and the Heuristic Learning blog are retained but explicitly flagged ("informally documented; cited as illustration, not evidence") in the text, in Table 1 (dagger marks), and in Appendix K. GEPA's venue is updated to ICLR 2026 (oral) per R2's R11. No claim now rests solely on an informal source.

The audit, kept visible. Following R2's R4 suggestion, the corrected "zero false accepts" is not silently fixed: §9 and Appendix H state that an earlier draft reported zero from a single seed while the theory's own Poisson prediction is ≈1.6 and the replayed seed gives 2 — "a one-seed illusion caught by the paper's own theorem." The Fig. 2b inset now shows the gated rate on a log scale instead of a line indistinguishable from zero.
